🛈 Curatorial Protocol & Technical Edge Cases

Documented guidelines for the festival committee

1
Director Identity Shield

Director account is strictly quarantined from matching pools. Candidates never see Director credentials or administrative logs.

2
Partial vs Full Reveal

Candidates receive anonymous Prom Night affinity cards prior to premiere. Instagram handles unlock upon gala verification.

3
Manual Unmatch Safeguard

Dissolving a pair revokes ticket passes and resets both cineastes to "In Review" status without data loss.

4
Free-Text Department Concordance

Departments accept any academic format (e.g. CSE S7, ECE S5). Affinity algorithms match mutual movie & music preferences automatically.
